Delete your Finator account

Delete directly in Finator or request deletion if you cannot access the app.

Deleting your Finator account is permanent. It does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

Delete your account in Finator

  1. Open Finator and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Find Account and device.
  4. Choose Delete Finator account.
  5. Read the explanation, confirm, and complete Google or Apple reauthentication.

Finator then removes the account’s primary cloud records and attempts to remove the corresponding local data and exclusive authentication record. A restricted, hashed retry receipt can remain temporarily if authentication cleanup needs to finish later.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot open or sign in to Finator, email finatorapp@gmail.com with the subject Finator account deletion request. State whether you used Google or Apple sign-in and provide the email address associated with that provider.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, encryption passwords, financial values, screenshots of financial records, or identity documents unless the publisher gives you a specific secure verification method.

The publisher will verify that you control the relevant account before deleting it. The target is to acknowledge a valid request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law. A request can take longer if ownership cannot be verified, a legal retention duty applies, or a service provider needs additional time.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes or schedules removal of the Finator owner’s supported cloud drafts, recovery points, saved decisions, notes, preferences, sync records, device leases, identity mappings, and server entitlement mirror. The app also attempts to remove that owner’s local Finator namespace on the device where the deletion is completed.

Data that can remain

  • Device-lease security events can remain until the 90-day retention job removes them; the owner link is removed during account deletion.
  • A completed hashed deletion retry receipt can remain for up to 30 days after authentication cleanup. An incomplete receipt remains until cleanup can finish.
  • Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Supabase logs, transaction records, and backups can remain for their own legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, and recovery periods.
  • Exported files, shared PDFs, encrypted backups or transfers, recipient copies, and operating-system or cloud-device backups are outside Finator’s control.
  • A shared underlying Google or Apple identity record can remain where deleting it would affect another Finator owner; Finator data for the deleted owner is still removed.

Subscription cancellation is separate

Deleting a Finator account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel through the store that billed you. Premium can remain active through the store-verified end of the paid period after cancellation.

Deletion contact

Use the email action to start a request if you cannot delete the account in Finator.
