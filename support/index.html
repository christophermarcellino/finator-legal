Finator support

Help with Finator’s account, subscription, backup, restore, and data controls.

For help, email finatorapp@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, Finator version, and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, encryption passwords, or screenshots containing private financial information.

Sign-in

Finator supports Google sign-in and, on iPhone, Apple sign-in. Guest mode does not require an account. If sign-in fails, confirm that the device is online, fully close and reopen Finator, and try once more.

In a support request, include the exact error text and the version shown under Settings → App diagnostics. Do not include provider tokens or account credentials.

Subscriptions

Open Settings → Current plan to view server-verified subscription status, refresh it, restore a purchase, or open the App Store or Google Play subscription manager.

Choosing Cancel subscription opens the store controls. Complete cancellation in the store that billed you. Deleting Finator or your Finator account does not cancel a store subscription.

Backup and restore

Encrypted files require the password used when they were created. Finator does not store or recover that password. Validate and preview a file before confirming an import. Privacy JSON exports and PDFs are not password-protected.

Delete saved data or an account

Delete individual decisions from their management controls. Delete all local data from Settings. Delete a signed-in account under Settings → Account and device → Delete Finator account.

If the app cannot be used, follow the public account-deletion request instructions.

Important limits

Finator provides educational calculations from user-entered assumptions. It does not provide credit, execute transactions, verify lender approval, or guarantee an outcome. Review original documents and consult an appropriately qualified professional when needed.

Contact support

Describe the problem without including passwords or private financial information.
