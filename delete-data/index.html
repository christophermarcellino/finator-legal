Delete your Finator cloud data

Delete supported cloud-synced financial records while keeping your Finator account active.

This is different from deleting your Finator account. Using this flow keeps your Finator account, Google or Apple sign-in, and subscription records.

Delete cloud data in Finator

  1. Open Finator and sign in to the account whose cloud data you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Find Account and device.
  4. Choose Turn off and delete cloud data.
  5. Review the information shown in the app and complete the confirmation steps.

This turns off cloud synchronization for the account and deletes the supported synced financial and user-content records covered by the flow. Your Finator account remains active.

Data deleted from Finator cloud storage

The cloud-data deletion flow removes supported synced financial and user-content records associated with your Finator account. Depending on what you previously saved or synchronized, this can include:

  • calculator assumptions, financial values, and results;
  • cloud-synced drafts and recovery points;
  • saved decisions and offer details;
  • notes and statuses; and
  • supported preferences stored as part of synchronized user content.

Source images and documents selected for offer review are processed on the device and are not uploaded to Finator cloud storage as source files. Extracted text or reviewed values can be included in a supported synced record and are deleted when that synced record is deleted.

Data that remains

Deleting cloud-synced financial data does not delete your Finator account. Information needed to keep the account and related services operating can remain, including:

  • your Finator account and internal user identifier;
  • Google or Apple sign-in information needed to authenticate the account;
  • subscription, transaction, entitlement, and related purchase records needed to provide or verify your subscription;
  • account-security information, device-access records, and service metadata needed to operate and protect the account; and
  • provider logs, transaction records, backups, or other records retained by Supabase, Google, Apple, RevenueCat, or other service providers for applicable legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or recovery purposes.

Some security and operational records follow the retention periods described in the Finator privacy policy. For example, device-lease security events become eligible for deletion after 90 days, and expired identity-link challenges become eligible after 24 hours.

Local data on your device

This page covers deletion of cloud-synced data. Data stored locally on your device is controlled separately. You can delete individual items or use Finator’s local-data controls in Settings to delete local data.

Files that you exported or shared, including privacy JSON exports, PDFs, encrypted backups or transfers, recipient copies, and operating-system or cloud-device backups, are outside Finator’s control and are not deleted by this cloud-data flow.

Request cloud-data deletion without the app

If you cannot use the in-app flow, email finatorapp@gmail.com with the subject Finator cloud data deletion request. State that you want your cloud-synced Finator data deleted while keeping your Finator account active, and provide the email address associated with your Google or Apple sign-in.

Finator may need to verify that you control the relevant account before acting. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, encryption passwords, financial values, screenshots of financial records, or identity documents unless you are given a specific secure verification method.

Account deletion is separate

If you want to delete your Finator account as well as its associated Finator data, use the account-deletion instructions.

Deleting cloud data or deleting a Finator account does not by itself cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription through the store that billed you.

Cloud-data deletion contact

Use the in-app control when available, or email a request if you cannot use the app.
