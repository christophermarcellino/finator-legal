Privacy policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · For adults aged 18 or older worldwide, subject to app-store availability and applicable law.

What Finator does

Finator is an educational financial decision-support application. It helps you compare choices using assumptions and information you provide. Calculations run on your device. Optional account features can store and synchronize supported records in the cloud.

Finator does not hold or transfer money, issue credit, connect users with lenders, execute transactions, or guarantee financial results.

Information Finator processes

Information that can remain on your device

Guest drafts, calculator inputs and results, recovery history, saved decisions, notes, preferences, and follow-up information remain in Finator’s local owner namespace unless you use an account feature that synchronizes a supported record.

Images and documents selected for offer review are processed on the device with native text recognition. Finator does not upload the source image or document bytes to its cloud storage. Extracted text or reviewed values that you include in a supported saved record can synchronize when account sync is used. Device file paths are removed from synchronized and portable records.

Information sent off the device

  • Account information: Google or Apple account identifiers and authentication tokens, an internal Finator user ID, and information the provider supplies, which can include your name and email address.
  • Account security information: a randomly generated app-installation ID, session and device-lease identifiers, platform, timestamps, identity-linking challenges, and security events used to protect an account and enforce the active-device rule.
  • Synced financial and user content: supported calculator assumptions, financial values, results, drafts, recovery points, saved decisions, offer details, notes, statuses, and preferences when cloud sync is available and used.
  • Purchase information: product, subscription, transaction, renewal, cancellation, refund, and entitlement information handled by Apple or Google, RevenueCat, and Finator’s server. Finator does not receive your complete payment-card or bank-account credentials from an app-store purchase.
  • Service metadata: providers can process IP addresses, timestamps, device and operating-system information, request metadata, and service logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Finator has no first-party advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting integration in the release source reviewed for this policy. It does not request contacts, microphone, or location access. Store and service providers can process operational data under their own notices.

Why information is used

Finator processes information to:

  • perform calculations and present explanations;
  • save, synchronize, export, restore, compare, and delete user-selected content;
  • create, authenticate, protect, link, and delete Finator accounts;
  • enforce device access and prevent replay, fraud, and unauthorized use;
  • provide, verify, restore, and manage subscriptions; and
  • diagnose operational failures without intentionally logging financial values, tokens, credentials, or source-file contents.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is performed to provide the service you request, with your consent for optional features, to comply with law, and for legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, support, and reliable service operation. You can avoid cloud synchronization by using guest features and can revoke photo or camera permission in your device settings.

Service providers and disclosures

Finator uses service providers only for the functions described here:

  • Supabase: authentication, hosted database, Edge Functions, and account sync;
  • Google and Apple: sign-in and operating-system or app-store services;
  • RevenueCat: subscription receipt validation, entitlement administration, and purchase analytics provided through its dashboard; and
  • Expo Application Services: signed builds and over-the-air application updates. Application source and build metadata are processed during delivery; Finator does not intentionally send user financial records to EAS.

These providers may use subprocessors and process information in other countries. Finator does not sell personal information and does not disclose it for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking. Information may also be disclosed when needed to comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate misuse, or complete a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards.

Provider notices are available from Supabase, Google, Apple, RevenueCat, and Expo.

Storage, security, and international processing

Finator uses HTTPS for network connections and native secure storage for credentials. Local SQLite records and source documents rely on the operating system’s application sandbox and device protection; they are not protected by a separate Finator database-encryption layer. Cloud sync is not end-to-end encrypted, and authorized service operators can access hosted records when needed to operate or support the service.

New password-protected backup and transfer files use authenticated encryption. Privacy JSON exports and shared PDFs are plaintext. Passwords for encrypted portable files are not stored and cannot be recovered by Finator. Copies exported or shared by you are controlled by you and the recipient. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

The configured Supabase production and development projects were hosted in Seoul, South Korea when this policy was prepared. Other providers and subprocessors may process data in other locations. Where required, Finator will use the provider and contractual safeguards applicable to the selected release countries.

Retention

  • Guest and signed-in local data remains in its owner namespace until you delete an item, delete all local data, delete the Finator account, or remove the app. Signing out locks the namespace and does not erase it. Recovery history is limited to ten entries.
  • Synced records, synchronization tombstones, and mutation receipts remain for the account lifetime because they prevent stale or duplicate changes.
  • Account identity mappings and active device leases remain while required to operate the account and are removed or revoked during account deletion.
  • Device-lease security events become eligible for deletion after 90 days. Expired identity-link challenges become eligible after 24 hours.
  • Completed, hashed account-deletion retry receipts become eligible for deletion 30 days after authentication cleanup. Incomplete receipts remain until cleanup can finish.
  • Finator’s RevenueCat replay ledger and entitlement mirror remain for the account lifetime and are deleted with the Finator owner record. Providers can have separate legal, security, accounting, and recovery retention periods.
  • Temporary share files are removed on a best-effort basis. A transfer expires after seven days for Finator import purposes, but expiry does not erase copies stored by a sender, recipient, operating system, or backup provider.

Your choices and rights

You can use available guest features without an account, review and correct extracted values before saving, delete individual items, delete all local data, export the current owner snapshot, control camera or photo permission in device settings, and manage your subscription through Apple or Google.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. To make a request, email finatorapp@gmail.com. Finator may need to verify that you control the relevant account before acting and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Account deletion

In Finator, open Settings, find Account and device, choose Delete Finator account, and complete provider reauthentication. This removes the Finator owner’s primary cloud records and attempts the corresponding local and authentication cleanup.

If you cannot use the app, follow the account-deletion instructions. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, encryption passwords, or financial records by email.

Deleting a Finator account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel the subscription through the store that billed it. Finator cannot delete provider records that must be retained for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or backup-recovery purposes, or copies that you exported or shared.

Children

Finator is designed for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from a child. If you believe a child has provided information, contact finatorapp@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where required, providing notice in the app or store listing.
